Hotel App

A mobile iOS application for Roksolana Hotel, designed to simplify check-in and check-out with a digital key and give guests more control over their stay.

Overview

A simpler digital hotel experience

Roksolana Hotel consists of six buildings and offers around 250 rooms across different categories. With a large number of rooms, the check-in and check-out process can become time-consuming for both guests and hotel staff.

The Hotel Roksolana app lets guests manage reservations, pay for their stay, check in remotely, receive notifications, and access their room without assistance from reception. It also provides an overview of hotel facilities, floor plans, and individual room layouts.

Platform iOS mobile app
Focus Booking, check-in & digital key
Industry Hospitality
Core experience
Search

The search experience is kept simple and easy to understand. Guests can select their dates, the number of adults and children, and the number of rooms to see the available options. Hotel rooms are organized into clear categories to make comparing and booking easier.

Digital key

After payment and identity verification, guests can use the app to access their room. The digital key makes arrival more convenient and removes the need to collect a physical key or card at reception.

  • Skip the reception queue
  • Access the room directly from the app
  • Enjoy a simpler, keyless guest experience
Features

The app also includes floor plans and room layouts, helping guests understand the hotel and their accommodation before booking. Filters and sorting make it easier to find a suitable room and complete the booking and payment process.

Main screens

The presentation focuses on the core screens needed to demonstrate the main booking, guest service, and room-access scenarios.
