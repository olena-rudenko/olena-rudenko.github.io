THE CONTEXT

Designing the operational layer behind modern airline retailing.

Airline retailing is moving beyond traditional booking and servicing models toward more flexible, product-driven experiences.

FLYR’s Offer & Order platform gives airlines a foundation for modern retailing across offers, orders, products, servicing, and third-party content.

My work focuses on making this complexity understandable and actionable for the people who use these systems every day: airline agents, retail teams, and operational stakeholders.

MY ROLE

From complex airline workflows to clear product experiences.

As a Senior Product Designer, I work across the Offer & Order ecosystem, with my primary focus on Contact Center and retailing applications.

  • Design experiences for the Offer & Order Management System.
  • Design tools that help airline agents manage customer needs and servicing workflows.
  • Design retailing experiences for creating and managing airline products.
  • Translate complex airline workflows into clear interaction models.
  • Define user flows, information architecture, wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity experiences.
  • Conduct interviews, surveys, and usability testing.
  • Iterate designs based on research and customer feedback.
  • Collaborate closely with product, engineering, design system, and business teams.
